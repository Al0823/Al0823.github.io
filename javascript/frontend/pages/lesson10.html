Operator Precedence


Operator precedence determines the order in which operations are performed.

Multiplication and division have higher precedence than addition and subtraction.

Parentheses can change the order of execution.




JavaScript evaluates expressions from left to right unless precedence rules override this.

Understanding precedence prevents unexpected results in complex expressions.
